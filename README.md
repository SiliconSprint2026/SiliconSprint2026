## How to get started (all tracks)

1. **Read your track's README.** It lists the tools, the setup steps and the setup check.
   Do the setup **before** the event.
2. **Create your repository** with your track's **Use template** link:
   * owner: one team member's own GitHub account
   * name: **`ss26-<track>-<team-name>`**, all lowercase:

     | Track | Repository name | Example |
     | --- | --- | --- |
     | Digital Design | `ss26-digital-<team-name>` | `ss26-digital-bitflippers` |
     | Mixed Signal Design | `ss26-mixedsignal-<team-name>` | `ss26-mixedsignal-bitflippers` |
     | Hardware for AI | `ss26-aihw-<team-name>` | `ss26-aihw-bitflippers` |
     | Digital Signal Processing | `ss26-dsp-<team-name>` | `ss26-dsp-bitflippers` |

   * visibility: **Private**
3. **Add collaborators:** your teammates, plus the organizer account **`SiliconSprint2026`**
   (role Read is enough). The judges review your work through this account.
4. **Register** your team: the registration link will be shared on the WhatsApp channel below.
5. **Run the setup check** described in your track's README.

## Rules for all tracks

* **Do not fork.** Forks of public repositories are public and cannot be made private.
  Only private repositories created with **Use template** are accepted.
* Keep your repository **private** until the awards are announced. We check that every
  registered repository URL shows 404 to a logged-out visitor.
* Push often. At **code freeze (28 Nov, 5:00 PM IST)** you submit your final commit SHA
  (the link will be shared on the WhatsApp channel). The judges grade exactly that commit.
* No sharing of code between teams. Use external material only with attribution.
* **AI tools are allowed.** Teams must transparently disclose where and how AI was used.
* Track-specific rules are in each track's problem statement and README.
